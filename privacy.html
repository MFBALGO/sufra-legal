Sufra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Sufra (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a household meal-planning app. This policy explains what information the app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep this short and plain because we collect very little.

Who this policy is for

Sufra is designed for a household: one person who plans meals (the “planner”) and a cook who views the day’s menu on a kitchen tablet. This policy applies to everyone who uses the app.

What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work. We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind, and we never sell your data.

  • Email address — used to sign you in with a one-time code (OTP). It is your account identifier.
  • Account identifier — a unique ID assigned to your account so the app knows which household and dishes are yours.
  • Names you add — your display name and the names of household members and groupings you create.
  • Photos — any photo you attach to a dish.
  • Content you create — your dishes, ingredients, prep notes, menus, headcounts, and shopping lists.
  • Notification token — if you allow notifications, a push token for your device (issued by Apple or Google through Expo) so the app can send you the notifications described below. It is used for nothing else.

We do not collect your location, contacts, device identifiers (other than the notification token above), advertising identifiers, browsing history, or usage analytics.

How we use your information

We use the information only to provide the app’s features — to sign you in, save your dishes and meal plans, scale recipes to your headcount, show the day’s menu on the cook’s display, build your shopping list, and power the AI features described below. We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or analytics.

How your information is shared

We do not sell or rent your data. We share information only with the service providers that make the app work:

  • Supabase — secure cloud hosting and database where your account and content are stored (data centre in Tokyo, Japan).
  • Resend — delivers your one-time sign-in code to your email address (the email comes from login@sufraappksa.com).
  • Anthropic (Claude) — the AI service behind the features described in the next section. Only the text needed for the feature is sent; no account data is sent.
  • Expo — relays notifications to your device through Apple’s and Google’s push services. Only the notification text and your device’s push token are sent.
  • Apple and Google — distribute the app through the App Store and Google Play, and deliver notifications to your device.

These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Dishes you make public. A dish you publish to Explore can be seen by other Sufra users, and can also be opened by anyone who has its link on sufraappksa.com. That page shows the dish’s name, photo and recipe, never your name or your household. Make the dish private again and its link stops working within about a minute. To stop links being abused, the website briefly counts visits using a one-way scrambled form of the visitor’s IP address; it is used for nothing else.

AI features

Four features use Anthropic’s Claude AI service (Anthropic PBC, a US company) to read text or photos for you, or to write text for you. In each case only what that feature needs is sent — never your email address, account identifier, or the names of household members:

  • Create with AI — the text you type into the Create with AI chat is sent to Claude to draft a dish for you to review (along with the list of measurement units you use, so the draft uses units you already have). It is used only to produce that draft. Sufra does not store the chat; only a dish you choose to save is kept, like any other dish you create.
  • Import a dish from a link — when you paste a recipe page, YouTube, TikTok or Instagram link, Sufra fetches that page’s or video’s public text (its recipe, description or caption) and sends it to Claude to pull out the recipe. You review the result before saving.
  • Kitchen-display translation — if the cook’s display is set to a different language from the one a dish was written in, the dish’s name, ingredient names and prep notes may be sent to Claude to translate them for the cook.
  • Snap a recipe — when you take or choose a photo of a recipe (for example a cookbook page or a handwritten recipe card), Sufra makes the photo smaller on your phone and sends it to Claude to read the recipe into a draft dish for you to review. The photo is used only to produce that draft: Sufra does not store it and does not keep it as the dish’s photo. If you later make a dish public, please only share recipes you have the right to share.

Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, text and photos sent this way are not used to train AI models, and Anthropic deletes it from its systems within 30 days unless it must keep it longer to enforce its usage policy or comply with the law. AI drafts and translations can contain mistakes — please check them before you rely on them. Every AI draft has a “Report a problem with this draft” link that emails us.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Sufra may send you:

  • Kitchen updates — for example when the menu is saved, and one “meal ready” message when the cook has finished a whole meal (for example “Lunch is ready”). You can switch the meal-ready message off in More → Meal ready.
  • A weekly planning reminder — on Thursday evening, if your household has planned before and next week is still mostly empty. At most once a week. You can switch it off in More → Weekly planning reminder.

You can turn off all notifications at any time in your phone’s settings. Notifications contain only short app text — never your email address.

How we protect your information

Sign-in uses a one-time code, the cook’s display is connected with a short, time-limited pairing code, and your content is stored in a database with per-household access rules so one household cannot see another’s data. Photos are served through short-lived private links.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as you use the app. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from inside the app (More → Delete account). You can also request deletion by emailing us at the address below, and we will remove it.

Children’s privacy

Sufra is intended for adults managing a household. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at this address.
